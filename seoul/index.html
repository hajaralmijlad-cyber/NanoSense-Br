Meet the fabric

The fabric is the sensor.

Dust touches the fabric. The fabric makes its own signal. No external power at the sensing element.

1

Dust touches the fabric

Every particle that hits the filter bag is an event the fabric can feel.

2

Fabric makes its own signal

The sensing layer turns that contact into an electrical signal, with no battery or wiring at the element.

3

AI reads the signal pattern

The model classifies each reading as Normal, Clogging or Rupture.

4

Right alert, right compartment

You see which compartment needs attention, before it becomes a shutdown.
